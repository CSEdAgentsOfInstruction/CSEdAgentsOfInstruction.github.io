# About these materials

These materials are adapted from **Aalto OpenCS Introduction to Programming**, parts 1–3, for you to explore and adapt during the tutorial. Tests and reference solutions are included to help you inspect exercises and revise them with your chosen AI agent.

Please retain the attribution and [course content notice](CONTENT-NOTICE.txt) when adapting the materials. For questions about reuse or sharing beyond the tutorial, ask the workshop facilitators.
